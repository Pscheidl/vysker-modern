Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: single high fidelity DESKTOP HOMEPAGE screenshot of a new Czech municipal website, LIGHT THEME.
Primary request: Design an exceptionally beautiful, genuinely ultra-modern civic website for Vyskeř, inspired by the precision of Vercel/Geist and the quiet, restrained surface hierarchy of Linear's 2026 interface refresh, combined with an original sculptural landscape identity. This is an ambitious new art direction, not a recolor of a previous design. It must feel like a high-end Silicon Valley technology studio designed a real usable public website. NOT a generic government template, not a crypto/AI dashboard. The page is in Czech.
Image framing: entire image is the actual flat web page screenshot, about 1600px wide by 1150px high. No browser chrome, no device frames, no presentation labels. Extremely sharp, legible text, pixel-perfect product design.
Visual system: very pale warm mineral-white canvas, dark graphite text, quiet silver borders, saturated periwinkle-violet #6547ED as the sole main accent, occasional pale lilac tint. Geist-like confident sans serif typography with carefully controlled weight and generous line height. Large proportional letterforms paired with tiny restrained monospace date labels. Smooth corners mostly 12-18px, subtle recessed controls and small tactile raised surfaces. Slight glass only in the navigation or search field, never behind document text. No green or blue government color scheme, no neon outlines, no blue/purple aurora gradients, no excessive boxed cards, no newspaper aesthetic.
Layout, from top to bottom:
1. A thin, calm header spanning the page, generous side gutters of about 64px. Left wordmark 'Vyskeř' and a small abstract three-line hill glyph. Center plain navigation 'Přehled' (active), 'Úřední deska', 'Dokumenty', 'Obec a úřad'. Right a small sun/moon theme control and a 'Kontakt ↗' text link. Underneath a nearly invisible divider.
2. A striking SHORT panoramic identity section, approximately 280px tall. HUGE beautifully typeset 'Vyskeř.' at left, dark graphite, about 100px. Under it small 'Obec v Českém ráji'. Across the remaining two-thirds, on the same seamless light canvas, an exquisite 3D relief sculpture of a hill and abstract village: layered matte white contours with sparse tiny architectural volumes, softly lit silver/ceramic edges, a thin lilac ribbon tracing a path, one small sculpted chapel as a generic symbol of place. It looks like a premium physical architectural model, NOT a real map or a game map, no false place labels, no pins or coordinates. The relief integrates into the blank background and remains visually subordinate to navigation and content. Small label at upper right '29. září 2026'. No welcome slogan.
3. Across the full content width, one long, white inset search field, magnifier and text 'Co hledáte?', right-side keyboard hint 'Ctrl K'. Under it a row of FOUR simple well-spaced service shortcuts on the unboxed page background, with thin contemporary icons and exact labels 'Úřední deska', 'Dokumenty a formuláře', 'Úřední hodiny', 'Odpady a poplatky'. Broad clickable targets, no big pastel capsules, no extra hero buttons.
4. Central full-width notice area titled 'Úřední deska', a small '3 vyvěšené' label, and a quiet segmented control 'Aktuální' / 'Archiv', active tab violet. Right aligned link 'Všechny dokumenty ↗'. Below, THREE generous horizontally aligned document previews in an exceptionally clean shared surface. Each column separated only by very soft vertical rules, no hard outlined tiles or shadows. Each has tiny category and posting-date metadata, a bold readable 20px two-line title, and clear footer with takedown date and right arrow.
Column 1: label 'ZASTUPITELSTVO', date '29. 9. 2026', title 'Zasedání zastupitelstva obce', footer 'Sejmutí 14. 10. 2026'.
Column 2: label 'HOSPODAŘENÍ', date '25. 9. 2026', title 'Rozpočtové opatření č. 3/2026', footer 'Sejmutí 10. 10. 2026'.
Column 3: label 'MAJETEK OBCE', date '20. 9. 2026', title 'Záměr pronájmu obecního pozemku', footer 'Sejmutí 5. 10. 2026', small understated amber badge 'Končí za 6 dní'. Clear document titles dominate metadata.
5. Lower horizontal strip on a subtly lilac-tinted surface, long form composition: left envelope icon, headline 'Nové dokumenty do e-mailu', short line 'Odběr aktivujete potvrzením e-mailové adresy.' Right one input visibly labelled 'E-mailová adresa', with placeholder 'vas@email.cz', and a violet rectangular button 'Odebírat'. Comfortable spacing, confident primary accent.
6. Quiet bottom row with 'Dění v obci' on left, two concise typographic previews '10. října · Podzimní setkání sousedů' and '12. října · Svoz bioodpadu' and a calendar link. Finish with a tiny neutral footer 'Obec Vyskeř' and 'Ilustrační návrh'.
Requirements: impeccable Czech accents. Correct town name 'Vyskeř'. No invented coat of arms, no personal names, no invented telephone numbers. No marketing slogans. Large readable text and obvious actions, all real information high contrast on solid surfaces. One carefully art-directed 3D object provides visual novelty and depth. Real content gets the majority of the page. This must be both visually surprising and plausibly implementable as an actual website.
